### Master The Infinite Floating Track
**Drift Boss** tests your spatial timing to the absolute limit. Your vehicle drives automatically along a narrow, suspended 3D track. With just a single button press, your car drifts smoothly to the right, and releasing the button swings you back to the left.

---

### Pro Tips
- **Anticipate Curves Early**: Press to drift just before the bend begins.
- **Collect Coins & Boosters**: Unlock dozens of customizable vehicles, each with unique visual styles and handling feel.
- **Watch Out For Bumps & Ramps**: Timing changes dramatically as track sections narrow or incorporate elevation jumps.
