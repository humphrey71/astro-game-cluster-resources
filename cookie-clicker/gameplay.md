### The Original Idle Game Legend
Created by Orteil in 2013, **Cookie Clicker** single-handedly defined and popularized the entire incremental/idle gaming genre. You start with a single click on a giant cookie to earn 1 cookie, which quickly snowballs into an absurdly colossal baking empire spanning grandmas, farms, mines, time machines, and antimatter condensers.

---

### How to Play & Upgrade
- **Click**: Tap the giant cookie to produce your initial currency.
- **Hire Grandmas & Buildings**: Automate your production per second (CpS).
- **Research Upgrades**: Unlock hundreds of tiered boosts to multiply your output exponentially.
- **Ascend**: When production slows down, ascend to gain permanent Heavenly Chips and prestige multipliers.
