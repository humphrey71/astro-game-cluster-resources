## Overview

Blockudoku blends block placement with sudoku-style board awareness. Instead of only thinking about long rows, you also need to watch smaller zones and keep the board balanced.

## Why It Is Strategic

Every shape changes the board. Strong players avoid panic clears and focus on placements that leave several future options open.

## How to Play

Place blocks into empty spaces, clear completed areas, and keep the board from locking up. There is no need to rush; good moves matter more than fast moves.

## FAQ

### Is Blockudoku harder than Block Blast?

It can be. Blockudoku often rewards more careful board planning.
