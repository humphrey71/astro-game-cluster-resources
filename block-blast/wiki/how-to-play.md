## Getting Started with Block Blast
If you are new to Block Blast, don't worry! The mechanics are incredibly intuitive. However, getting a high score requires learning a few fundamental tricks.

### The Game Interface
- **The Board**: An 8x8 grid that starts completely empty.
- **The Hand**: Three block shapes generated at the bottom. You must place all three before receiving the next set.

### Basic Placement Rules
- **No Rotation**: You cannot rotate any blocks. They must fit exactly as they appear in your hand.
- **Line Clearing**: You clear blocks by forming solid, uninterrupted horizontal or vertical lines.
- **Drop & Drag**: Drag blocks using your mouse or touch screen. Once released, they are locked in place.

### Three Golden Rules for Beginners
1. **Always Check Next Blocks**: Before placing the first block in your hand, check the remaining two. Ensure you don't block placements for them.
2. **Clear Lines Early**: Don't let blocks pile up. Keep the center of the grid as open as possible.
3. **Build Combos**: Try to clear lines in consecutive moves. This activates combo multipliers that boost your score exponentially!
