## Start With Space Management

The easiest way to improve at Block Blast is to protect open space. A clear board gives you options, and options keep your run alive.

## Use Corners Carefully

Corners are useful for square blocks, but they can become traps when you fill them too early. Try to keep at least one side of the board flexible.

## Think One Set Ahead

You cannot predict every future shape, but you can avoid placements that only work if a perfect piece appears next.
