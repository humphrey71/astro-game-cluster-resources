### Core Gameplay Mechanics
Block Blast uses a simple but highly strategic puzzle system.

#### 1. 8×8 Grid Board
The game is played on an 8×8 board where blocks must be placed to form complete rows or columns.

#### 2. Three Blocks Per Turn
Each round provides three different block shapes. You must place all three blocks before the next set appears.

#### 3. Line Clearing
When a row or column is completely filled, it disappears and awards points.

#### 4. No Rotation
Blocks cannot be rotated, which adds an extra layer of challenge when deciding where to place each piece.

#### 5. Game Over Condition
The game ends when there is no space left on the board for the available blocks.

### Tips to Get a High Score in Block Blast
At first glance, Block Blast looks like a very simple puzzle game. But if you want to achieve really high scores, a little strategy goes a long way:

* **Think ahead before placing blocks**: Instead of dropping the first block you see, take a moment to look at all three available shapes.
* **Keep the middle of the board clear**: The center of the grid is the most flexible area. When it stays open, you’ll have more options for large shapes.
* **Avoid leaving tiny gaps**: Small one-square holes quickly become difficult to fill, limiting your available moves.
* **Leave room for bigger blocks**: Keep a few open areas so you don't get stuck when large pieces appear.
* **Try to clear multiple lines at once**: Clearing a row and a column together yields combo points!
