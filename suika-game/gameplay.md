### Play Suika Game (Watermelon Game) Online For Free
**Suika Game**, also known globally as the Watermelon Game, is the sensational Japanese puzzle sensation that took the internet by storm. In this relaxing yet deeply strategic physics-based merging game, your goal is to drop various fruits into a container and combine identical ones to evolve them into larger fruits, ultimately aiming to create the legendary Watermelon.

---

### Core Gameplay & Merging Order
Every time two matching fruits collide, they merge into the next tier:
1. **Cherry** -> **Strawberry** -> **Grapes**
2. **Dekopon (Orange)** -> **Persimmon** -> **Apple**
3. **Pear** -> **Peach** -> **Pineapple**
4. **Melon** -> **Giant Watermelon**!

### Key Strategies for High Scores
- **Stack by Size**: Keep smaller fruits on top and avoid trapping tiny cherries beneath massive pineapples.
- **Utilize Physics & Rolling**: Anticipate how curved fruits will roll and bounce when dropped.
- **Prevent Overflow**: The game ends as soon as any fruit crosses the top boundary line of the box.
