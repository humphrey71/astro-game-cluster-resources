### The Ultimate Creative Physics Playground
**Melon Sandbox** (formerly Melon Playground) is a beloved 2D physics sandbox inspired by classic physics mods. It provides players with an enormous sandbox toy box of ragdoll characters, weapons, vehicles, syringes, and kinetic mechanisms.

---

### What You Can Do
- **Ragdoll Physics**: Experiment with realistic skeletal deformation, gravity, and kinetic impact.
- **Build Complex Machines**: Connect gears, ropes, thrusters, and pistons to craft working elevators, cars, or catapults.
- **Pure Sandbox Freedom**: No set goals, no timers, and no limits—create your own custom scenarios freely.
