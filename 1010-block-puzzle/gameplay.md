## Overview

1010 Block Puzzle uses a clean 10x10 board and simple block-placement rules. It is approachable for beginners while still leaving room for deep planning and long scoring runs.

## How to Play

Place shapes onto the board and complete full lines to clear them. The game ends when none of the available pieces can fit.

## Tips

Keep large sections of the board open, avoid scattered holes, and build clears that make room for future pieces.

## FAQ

### What is the goal?

The goal is to score as high as possible before the board runs out of space.
