## Overview

Wood Block Puzzle brings the classic block-placement formula into a warmer visual style. The rules are easy to understand: place shapes, complete lines, and keep enough empty space for the next round.

## What Makes It Different?

The wooden theme makes the game feel slower and more relaxed than flashier puzzle games. It is still strategic, but it works especially well when you want a calm browser game with clear feedback and simple goals.

## How to Play

Place each wooden block shape on the board. Completed rows or columns clear automatically. Continue placing pieces until the board no longer has enough room.

## Strategy Tips

- Keep long horizontal and vertical lanes open.
- Do not fill corners too quickly.
- Save flexible gaps for awkward shapes.
- Look for placements that support both row and column clears.

## FAQ

### Is Wood Block Puzzle timed?

No. You can think through each move at your own pace.

### Is it similar to Block Blast?

Yes. Both games use block placement and line clearing, but Wood Block Puzzle has a calmer style.
