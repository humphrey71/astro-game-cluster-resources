### Build Your Thriving Supermarket Empire
**Monkey Mart** is a charming and deeply engaging 3D management simulation developed by TinyDobbins. Step into the shoes of a hardworking monkey entrepreneur running a supermarket from the ground up!

---

### Gameplay Flow
- **Plant & Harvest**: Grow bananas, corn, and wheat to supply your grocery aisles.
- **Serve Customers**: Keep shelves stocked and man the checkout counters to collect coins.
- **Hire Employees**: Recruit monkey assistants and cashiers to automate your bustling enterprise.
- **Expand New Aisle Branches**: Unlock bakeries, dairy bars, and premium specialty departments.
