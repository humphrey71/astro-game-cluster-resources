### What is Friday Night Funkin?
**Friday Night Funkin' (FNF)** is a popular open-source rhythm game where you play as Boyfriend and try to win a singing contest against your Girlfriend's dad and many other colorful opponents. It features catchy music tracks, unique retro art, and challenging fast-paced gameplay.

---

### How to Play FNF?
The gameplay is easy to grasp but hard to master:
1. **Match the Arrows**: As the notes rise up, press the corresponding arrow keys (or WASD keys) at the exact moment they overlap with the targets at the top.
2. **Keep the Rhythm**: Missing notes will shift the health bar towards your opponent. If the bar empties, it's Game Over!
3. **Be Victorious**: Survive until the end of the song to progress to the next week's challenge.
