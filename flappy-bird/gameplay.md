### Play The Unforgettable Classic
**Flappy Bird** is the world-famous arcade tap-and-fly trial created by Dong Nguyen that captured global headlines. With deceptively simple one-button mechanics and punishing hitboxes, it offers one of the purest, most thrilling reflex challenges in mobile gaming history.

---

### Controls & Survival Tips
- **One-Tap Control**: Click or tap anywhere to flap upward; gravity will constantly pull you downward.
- **Find the Rhythm**: Do not spam taps. Master small, measured wing beats right as you approach the bottom of pipe gaps.
- **Patience Over Panic**: Keep your eyes focused two pipes ahead to prepare for varying vertical elevations.
