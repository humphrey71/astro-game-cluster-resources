## Overview

Jewel Block Puzzle adds bright gem-style visuals to the familiar block puzzle loop. It is easy to learn, browser friendly, and useful for short sessions when you want a quick puzzle challenge.

## Gameplay

Place jewel blocks on the grid and complete full rows or columns. Each clear opens space and keeps the round alive. The goal is to manage the board for as long as possible.

## How to Improve

Strong scores come from preserving open areas, clearing lines before the board gets crowded, and avoiding gaps that only one rare shape can fill.
